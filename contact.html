Frequently asked questions

How quickly will someone get back to me?
Messages sent through this form reach a US-based specialist, not a ticket queue. We reply within one business day, and usually the same afternoon if you write before 3PM EST. If your request is urgent, call the support line instead — it is staffed 24/7.
What should I have ready for a rate quote?
Three months of recent processing statements is all it takes. From those we can read your effective rate, the card mix you actually take and any fees buried in the monthly totals, then show you the same volume priced on our interchange-plus model. No statements yet? Tell us your monthly volume and average ticket and we will estimate from published card-present averages.
Do I need to switch banks to work with PayNexa?
No. Your deposits keep landing in the business account you already use — we only need the routing and account numbers to set up funding. Nothing about your existing banking relationship, credit line or bookkeeping has to change.
