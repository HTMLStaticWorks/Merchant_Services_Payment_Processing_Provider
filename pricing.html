FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Interchange Plus pricing?
Interchange Plus is the most transparent pricing model in the payment industry. "Interchange" is the wholesale cost set by card networks (Visa, Mastercard, etc.). The "Plus" is a small, fixed markup that PayNexa charges for processing the transaction. Unlike flat-rate pricing, you pay the exact wholesale cost of each card type, which usually results in significant savings for established businesses.
Are there any long-term contracts?
No. All PayNexa agreements are month-to-month. If you decide we're not the right fit, you can close your account at any time without paying early termination or cancellation fees.
How do chargebacks work and what are the fees?
If a customer disputes a charge, a non-refundable $15 chargeback fee applies. However, our dashboard provides an automated dispute resolution center where you can easily upload evidence to fight and win chargebacks.
When do I get my money?
We offer Next-Day Funding for all standard accounts. Transactions batched before 11:00 PM EST will be deposited into your linked business bank account the very next morning.
Do I have to buy new hardware?
Not necessarily. Our systems are compatible with over 90% of existing POS terminals and gateways on the market. If you need new hardware, we offer a range of smart terminals at wholesale prices.
